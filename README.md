## 🌺 What is Ara?

Ara is your creative co-pilot for building, composing, and orchestrating AI agents. Imagine a pastel sky at golden hour — soft pinks, warm corals, a little shimmer of lavender — that's Ara's visual soul, the **pink-aeroplane identity**: bold enough to lead, dreamy enough to inspire.

Ara doesn't just run tasks. She *understands* them. She learns, adapts, and helps you ship agent workflows that feel like magic. Whether you're wiring together tools, designing multi-step pipelines, or launching something entirely new — Ara is your co-builder, your creative partner, your favourite passenger on this flight. 💕

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) and let the pink magic begin. 🌸
