# Security Policy

Ara takes security reports seriously and appreciates coordinated disclosure from researchers, customers, and community members.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security reports to security@ara.so. Include the affected repository or product area, a clear description, reproduction steps, impact, and any proof-of-concept material that is safe to share.

Please do not open public issues for suspected vulnerabilities. Ara will acknowledge valid reports as quickly as possible, triage severity, and coordinate remediation and disclosure timelines with the reporter when appropriate.

## Scope

This policy covers Ara-owned public repositories, Ara desktop and web product surfaces, and supporting services operated by Ara. Repository-specific security files may define additional scope, contacts, or supported versions.

## Responsible disclosure expectations

- Give Ara a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before public disclosure.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, or exfiltrating data that does not belong to you.
- Use test accounts and non-destructive proof-of-concepts whenever possible.
- Stop testing and report immediately if you encounter sensitive data or service instability.

## Out of scope

Social engineering, phishing, physical attacks, spam, denial-of-service testing, automated high-volume scanning, and reports that only describe missing best-practice headers without exploitability are out of scope unless Ara has explicitly authorized the activity in writing.

## Supported versions

Public repositories are generally supported from their default branch unless a repository-specific policy says otherwise. Archived repositories are unsupported except for critical reports that materially affect active Ara systems.
