# Security Policy

Ara takes security reports seriously and appreciates coordinated disclosure from researchers, customers, and contributors.

## Supported scope

This repository is the organization profile and governance baseline package for Ara-managed open source projects. It covers shared community files, brand assets, and default governance policies that downstream repositories may reference.

For product or application vulnerabilities, report the affected Ara service, repository, account, or workflow with enough detail for the team to reproduce and triage the issue.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@ara.so with:

- A concise description of the issue and impact.
- Reproduction steps, proof-of-concept details, logs, screenshots, or affected URLs when safe to share.
- Whether you believe customer data, credentials, billing, infrastructure, or production availability may be affected.
- Your preferred contact information for follow-up and credit.

If security@ara.so is unavailable, contact sven@ara.so and include "Security report" in the subject.

## Disclosure expectations

Please give Ara a reasonable window to investigate, mitigate, and coordinate disclosure before publishing details. We aim to acknowledge credible reports within 3 business days and provide status updates as the investigation progresses.

## Out of scope

Do not perform testing that causes service disruption, data destruction, social engineering, spam, phishing, physical attacks, or denial-of-service activity. Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data that is not your own.
