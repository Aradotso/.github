# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in this project, please report it responsibly by emailing **security@ara.so**. Do not open a public GitHub issue for security-related matters.

Please include as much detail as possible: a description of the vulnerability, steps to reproduce, potential impact, and any suggested mitigations.

## Response SLA

- **Acknowledgement**: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within **2 business days**.
- **Status update**: We will provide a meaningful status update — including an initial assessment and next steps — within **7 days** of your report.

## Coordinated Disclosure Timeline

We follow a **90-day coordinated disclosure window** from the date of the initial report. During this period, we ask that you refrain from publicly disclosing details of the vulnerability while we work to understand and remediate the issue.

If a vulnerability is critical in nature and active exploitation is likely or confirmed, we may coordinate an earlier disclosure in collaboration with the reporting researcher to protect users.

## Responsible Disclosure Pledge

We are committed to working collaboratively and in good faith with security researchers. In return for responsible disclosure, we pledge to:

- **Not pursue legal action** against researchers who discover and report vulnerabilities in accordance with these responsible disclosure guidelines.
- **Credit researchers** in our security advisories for their discovery (with their permission).
- **Work collaboratively** to fully understand and fix the issue before any public disclosure.
- Treat all reports with confidentiality and respect.

We ask that researchers similarly act in good faith: avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability, and refrain from disrupting services or other users.

## Thank You

Security researchers play a vital role in keeping Ara and its users safe. We genuinely appreciate the time and effort you put into responsible disclosure.
