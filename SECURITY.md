# Security Policy

Ara takes security reports seriously and appreciates responsible disclosure.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@ara.so with a clear description of the issue, affected repository or product area, reproduction steps, impact, and any relevant proof of concept. Do not open a public issue for suspected vulnerabilities.

Please avoid accessing, modifying, deleting, or exfiltrating data that is not yours. Give Ara a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before sharing details publicly.

## What to expect

Ara will acknowledge credible reports as quickly as possible, usually within two business days. We will investigate, prioritize by severity and exploitability, and share remediation status when appropriate.

## Scope

This policy applies to public repositories in the Ara GitHub organization and publicly reachable Ara services. Third-party services, customer environments, social engineering, spam, denial-of-service testing, and physical attacks are out of scope unless Ara has explicitly authorized them in writing.

## Safe handling

Never include secrets, tokens, customer data, personal data, or exploit payloads in public issues, pull requests, discussions, screenshots, logs, or commits.
