# Ara Code of Conduct

Ara is building a community where people can create, share, and improve agents safely. We want every contributor, user, maintainer, partner, and visitor to feel welcome, respected, and able to participate without harassment.

This Code of Conduct applies in Ara-owned community spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, events, chat spaces, social channels, and any other forum where someone is participating as part of the Ara community.

## Our community values

We ask everyone in the Ara community to help uphold these values:

- Be welcoming. Make room for newcomers, people learning in public, and contributors from different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Be respectful. Assume good intent, listen carefully, and disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Be constructive. Keep feedback specific, actionable, and focused on improving the work or the community.
- Be safe. Avoid behavior that could intimidate, exclude, endanger, or expose private information about others.
- Be accountable. If you make a mistake, acknowledge it, repair harm where possible, and learn from the experience.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Using inclusive language and respecting names, pronouns, identities, cultures, and boundaries.
- Giving credit for ideas, code, designs, documentation, and community work.
- Asking before sharing private conversations, screenshots, recordings, or personal information.
- Accepting maintainer decisions about scope, moderation, and project direction with professionalism.
- Helping keep issues and discussions relevant, actionable, and useful for the wider community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment and abusive behavior are not tolerated. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Insults, slurs, demeaning comments, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Harassment based on age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, socioeconomic status, or any other protected or personal characteristic.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or inappropriate advances.
- Threats, intimidation, stalking, doxxing, or sharing someone else's private information without consent.
- Trolling, baiting, repeated off-topic comments, spam, or other behavior that interferes with productive collaboration.
- Encouraging or assisting others in violating this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to Ara at conduct@ara.so.

Reports should include as much context as you feel safe sharing, such as:

- What happened and where it happened.
- Links, screenshots, timestamps, or usernames related to the incident.
- Whether the behavior is ongoing or urgent.
- Any outcome or support you are requesting.

Ara will handle reports as confidentially as possible. We will not share the identity of a reporter without consent except when necessary to address an immediate safety risk or comply with legal obligations.

If there is an immediate threat to safety, contact local emergency services first.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers and community moderators are responsible for clarifying standards, responding to reports, and taking action when this Code of Conduct is violated. Enforcement may include:

1. Correction: A private or public request to stop the behavior, edit content, or follow community guidelines.
2. Warning: A formal warning explaining the violation and consequences for repeated behavior.
3. Temporary restriction: Temporary loss of access to issues, pull requests, discussions, events, or other Ara community spaces.
4. Permanent restriction: Permanent removal from Ara community spaces when behavior is severe, repeated, or unsafe.
5. Escalation: Referral to platform administrators, employers, legal authorities, or emergency services when required for safety or compliance.

Enforcement decisions will consider the impact of the behavior, the safety of affected people, prior conduct, and the likelihood of repair. Retaliation against anyone who reports an issue or participates in a review is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.

This file is intentionally placed at the repository root so GitHub's Community profile health dashboard can detect it and flag Ara's GitHub presence as having community safety guidance.

Maintainers should periodically review GitHub's Community profile health dashboard for this repository and update this file when policies, contacts, or enforcement practices change.
