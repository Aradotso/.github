# Code of Conduct

Ara expects everyone participating in our repositories, issues, discussions, reviews, events, and community spaces to help maintain a respectful, focused, and inclusive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while respecting impact.
- Keep discussion relevant to the work and product goals.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.
- Give and receive technical feedback without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, trolling, sustained disruption, impersonation, and abuse of private or confidential information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to conduct@ara.so. Include links, screenshots, dates, names or handles involved, and any context that helps Ara review the issue. If a conduct concern also involves a security vulnerability, follow SECURITY.md as well.

## Enforcement

Ara maintainers may remove comments, moderate discussions, limit participation, block users, or take other action they believe is necessary to protect the community and the work. Enforcement decisions consider severity, context, prior behavior, and impact.

## Scope

This code applies across Ara-owned public repositories and community spaces. Repository-specific rules may add stricter expectations.
