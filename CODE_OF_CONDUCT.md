# Code of Conduct

Ara communities should be useful, respectful, and safe for contributors, customers, researchers, and maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback that helps the work improve.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible disclosure boundaries.
- Make space for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, sustained disruption, doxxing, publishing private information, impersonation, spam, and abuse of project infrastructure or support channels.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to conduct@ara.so. If that address is unavailable, contact sven@ara.so with "Code of conduct report" in the subject.

Reports should include the relevant project or channel, people involved, links or screenshots when available, and any immediate safety concerns.

## Enforcement

Ara maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, block accounts, close issues or pull requests, or escalate severe incidents to the appropriate platform or legal channel. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented where appropriate, and focused on protecting the community.
