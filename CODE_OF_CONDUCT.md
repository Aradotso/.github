# Code of Conduct

Ara is committed to a respectful, focused, and inclusive environment for contributors, maintainers, customers, and community members.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Keep discussions focused on the work and the people affected by it.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Protect confidential information, personal data, and security-sensitive details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, sustained disruption, and disclosure of private or security-sensitive information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code. Ara may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for serious or repeated violations.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to conduct@ara.so. Security vulnerabilities should be reported through `SECURITY.md` instead of conduct channels.
