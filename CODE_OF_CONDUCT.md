# Code of Conduct

Ara is committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project
- Showing empathy toward other contributors and users

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or imagery
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Unwanted sexual attention or advances
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Ara maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to conduct@ara.so. Ara will review reports promptly and handle them with appropriate confidentiality.
