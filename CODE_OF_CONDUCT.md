# Ara Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Ara is building agentic tools for people who create, automate, and collaborate. We want every Ara community space to be welcoming, safe, and productive for contributors, users, partners, and maintainers.

We are committed to a harassment-free community experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political belief, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, socioeconomic status, veteran status, or any other protected or personal characteristic.

## Our values

Ara community members are expected to help create an environment grounded in:

- Respect: Assume good intent, listen actively, and make room for different perspectives.
- Safety: Protect people from harassment, threats, abuse, and unwanted attention.
- Craft: Share useful context, give constructive feedback, and improve the work together.
- Accountability: Own mistakes, repair harm where possible, and follow maintainer guidance.
- Inclusion: Welcome new contributors and avoid gatekeeping, exclusionary jokes, or hostile language.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Using welcoming, inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Disclosing conflicts of interest when relevant.
- Respecting privacy, consent, and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment and other exclusionary behavior are not tolerated. Examples include:

- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Public or private harassment, intimidation, stalking, or threats.
- Publishing someone else's private information without explicit permission.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs, stereotypes, or demeaning language.
- Unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Abuse of reporting channels, moderation processes, or maintainer access.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Ara community spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, events, support channels, social spaces, and any other venue where someone represents Ara or the Ara community.

It also applies when behavior outside Ara spaces affects the safety, trust, or participation of community members.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or if you have any safety concern, report it to Ara at conduct@ara.so.

Reports should include as much relevant detail as you are comfortable sharing, such as:

- What happened and when.
- Where it happened, including links or screenshots if available.
- Who was involved or witnessed it.
- Whether there is an immediate safety concern.

Ara maintainers will handle reports as promptly and confidentially as possible. We will only share information with people who need it to investigate, respond, or comply with legal obligations. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is not tolerated.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and impact of the behavior, maintainers may take any of the following actions:

1. Correction: A private or public clarification that the behavior was inappropriate, with an expectation that it stops.
2. Warning: A formal warning describing the violation and consequences for continued behavior.
3. Temporary restriction: A temporary ban from participating in Ara community spaces, repositories, events, or communication channels.
4. Permanent restriction: A permanent ban from Ara community spaces when behavior is severe, repeated, threatening, or unsafe.
5. Escalation: Contacting platform administrators, event security, employers, legal counsel, or law enforcement when required to protect people or comply with law.

Enforcement decisions will consider the facts available, the impact on affected people, prior behavior, safety risks, and the needs of the community. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.

## Appeals

A person subject to enforcement may request reconsideration by replying to the enforcement notice or contacting conduct@ara.so. Appeals should include new context, evidence, or remediation steps. Ara may decline appeals that are abusive, repetitive, or submitted in bad faith.
