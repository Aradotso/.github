# Ara Community Code of Conduct

## Our community values

Ara is building agentic software that helps people get real work done. We want every Ara community space to be welcoming, practical, and safe for people across backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and disciplines.

We ask everyone who participates in Ara projects, discussions, issues, pull requests, events, and other community spaces to:

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for your impact.
- Make space for people with different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Share feedback in ways that help people and the project improve.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and confidentiality.
- Put community safety ahead of speed, cleverness, or winning an argument.

## Anti-harassment policy

Ara does not tolerate harassment or abuse in any form. Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

- Offensive comments related to age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, physical appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, veteran status, or other protected characteristics.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or inappropriate advances.
- Threats, intimidation, stalking, sustained disruption, or deliberate personal attacks.
- Doxxing, sharing private information without consent, or pressuring others to reveal private information.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an enforcement process.

Community members asked to stop unacceptable behavior are expected to comply immediately.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, or if you are unsure whether something should be reported, contact the Ara team at [sven@ara.so](mailto:sven@ara.so).

Reports should include as much context as you feel safe sharing, such as links, screenshots, dates, usernames, and a short description of what happened. Ara will handle reports as privately as possible and will only share information with people who need it to review and respond.

If a report involves the listed contact, or you need another reporting path, contact Ara through [https://www.ara.so](https://www.ara.so) and ask for a community safety escalation.

## Enforcement guidelines

Ara maintainers and community moderators are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and taking fair, consistent action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Possible enforcement actions include:

- A private clarification or reminder about community expectations.
- A formal warning and request to change behavior.
- Removal, editing, or rejection of comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions.
- Temporary restriction from participating in Ara community spaces.
- Permanent removal from Ara community spaces.
- Escalation to platform administrators or legal authorities when necessary.

When reviewing a report, Ara will consider the impact of the behavior, the safety of affected people, relevant context, prior incidents, and whether the person responsible takes meaningful accountability.

## Community profile health

This `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` is placed at the repository root so GitHub can detect it in the Community profile health dashboard and flag the repository as community-friendly.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Ara project spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, community events, and other official or project-related channels. It also applies when someone represents Ara in public spaces.
