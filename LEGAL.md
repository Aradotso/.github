## Open-Source vs. Proprietary

Ara publishes a mix of open-source and proprietary software.

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **Proprietary** | Core products and services — including the Ara desktop app, Ara API, and associated backend infrastructure — are proprietary. Source code is confidential. All rights reserved. See [LICENSE](./LICENSE). |
| **Open-source** | Select libraries, SDKs, or sample projects published under the [Aradotso](https://github.com/Aradotso) GitHub organisation may carry their own open-source licence (e.g. MIT or Apache 2.0). Check the `LICENSE` file in the individual repository. |
| **Unlicensed** | Repositories that contain no `LICENSE` file are proprietary by default; no rights are granted. |

If you are unsure whether a specific project is open-source, check its repository `LICENSE` file or contact [legal@ara.so](mailto:legal@ara.so).

## Privacy and Data Handling

Ara's full Privacy Policy is available at [ara.so/privacy](https://www.ara.so/privacy).

### Summary

**Data we collect**  
When you use Ara products, we may collect information you provide (account details, messages), usage data (feature interactions, diagnostics), and technical data (device type, OS version, crash logs).

**How we use it**  
We use collected data to provide and improve our services, respond to support requests, and, where you have opted in, to personalise your experience.

**Data sharing**  
We do not sell personal data. We share data only with trusted sub-processors necessary to operate our services (e.g. cloud infrastructure, analytics), and only to the extent required by law or with your explicit consent.

**AI / LLM processing**  
Ara routes messages through AI models (including third-party providers). Conversation content may be processed by those providers subject to their data-processing agreements. We apply reasonable contractual safeguards. For enterprise or sensitive deployments, contact [privacy@ara.so](mailto:privacy@ara.so) to discuss data-handling options.

**Retention**  
We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide services or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting [privacy@ara.so](mailto:privacy@ara.so).

**Your rights**  
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Exercise these rights via [privacy@ara.so](mailto:privacy@ara.so).
