If a repository or module does not include an explicit open-source license file, it is proprietary and

## 3. Privacy and Data Handling

Ara's full Privacy Policy is published at https://ara.so/privacy. Key points:

- **Data collected**: Ara collects account information (email, name), usage telemetry, and content you
  submit to the service (prompts, agent outputs) as described in the Privacy Policy.
- **Data use**: Data is used to provide and improve the service, ensure security, and comply with legal
  obligations. Ara does not sell personal data to third parties.
- **Third-party processors**: Ara uses Supabase (database), Stripe (billing), and PostHog (analytics).
  Each processor operates under a Data Processing Agreement and applicable data-protection law.
- **Retention**: User data is retained as long as the account is active or as required by law. Users may
  request deletion at privacy@ara.so.
- **Security**: Ara encrypts data in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to production systems is restricted
  and audited.

For data-subject requests or privacy questions, contact privacy@ara.so.
