# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our services and products seriously. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to us responsibly. You can report security issues by:

* Sending an email to security@ara.so
* Submitting a report via GitHub's Security Advisory form under the Security tab of this repository.

Please include a detailed description of the issue, steps to reproduce, and any supporting material (such as screenshots or proof-of-concept code).

## Expected Response SLA

We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours and provide a preliminary assessment of the issue. We aim to resolve all verified high-severity vulnerabilities within 14 days and medium-to-low severity issues within 30 days.

## Scope

This security policy applies to all assets, repositories, and services owned and operated under the Aradotso organization on GitHub, including but not limited to the Ara platform services, CLI tools, and associated public repositories.

## Bug Bounty Program

At this time, we do not operate a public monetary bug bounty program. However, we deeply appreciate and recognize the contributions of security researchers who help keep our users safe. We will gladly acknowledge your assistance in our security advisories and release notes with your consent.
