# Ara Community Code of Conduct

Ara is building trustworthy agent infrastructure for people who work with software, design, research, operations, and automation. We want every community space connected to Ara to be useful, welcoming, and safe for contributors, users, partners, and maintainers.

This Code of Conduct applies to public Ara repositories, issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, community events, and other project spaces where people collaborate around Ara.

## Our values

We ask everyone in the Ara community to uphold these values:

- Be respectful. Treat people with dignity, even when disagreeing with their ideas or implementation choices.
- Be constructive. Share feedback that helps the work improve, and explain tradeoffs clearly.
- Be inclusive. Welcome people across backgrounds, identities, abilities, locations, and experience levels.
- Be responsible. Consider the safety, privacy, and consent implications of agentic software and automation.
- Be accountable. Own mistakes, correct harmful behavior, and help maintain a healthy collaboration environment.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing discussions on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respecting maintainers' time, review decisions, and security boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Ara does not tolerate harassment, abuse, intimidation, or exclusionary behavior. Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

- Offensive comments related to age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, socioeconomic status, or technology choices.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Public or private harassment, intimidation, stalking, or threats.
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information, such as physical addresses, credentials, personal contact details, or private communications, without explicit permission.
- Encouraging harmful automation, unsafe use of agents, credential exposure, abuse of third-party systems, or other behavior that puts people or services at risk.
- Any conduct that a maintainer reasonably determines to be inappropriate in a professional community setting.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or if you have a safety concern related to Ara community spaces, report it to the Ara team at sven@ara.so.

Please include as much context as you are comfortable sharing, such as:

- What happened and when.
- Where it happened, including links to issues, pull requests, discussions, or messages if available.
- Who was involved or affected.
- Whether there is an immediate safety, privacy, or security concern.

Reports will be handled with care and discretion. Ara will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy and safety of reporters and affected people. If the concern involves a security vulnerability or sensitive technical exploit, use the repository's security reporting process when one is available, or clearly mark the report as security-sensitive in your message.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and taking appropriate, fair corrective action in response to behavior they determine to be inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Enforcement actions may include:

1. Clarification or correction: A maintainer may privately or publicly clarify expectations and ask the person to adjust their behavior.
2. Warning: A maintainer may issue a formal warning for behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.
3. Temporary restriction: A maintainer may temporarily limit participation in issues, discussions, reviews, community spaces, or repository access.
4. Permanent restriction: A maintainer may permanently remove someone from community spaces or repository participation for severe, repeated, or unsafe conduct.
5. Escalation: When behavior involves credible threats, privacy violations, credential exposure, security abuse, or legal risk, Ara may escalate the matter to platform administrators, service providers, or other appropriate channels.

Enforcement decisions should be based on the impact and severity of the behavior, the safety of affected people, and the health of the project community. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in a review process is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## GitHub community health

GitHub uses community profile files such as `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` to evaluate whether a repository is community-friendly in the Community profile health dashboard. Keeping this file in the repository helps GitHub recognize Ara's community standards and gives contributors a clear place to find Ara's values, reporting path, and enforcement expectations.

This Code of Conduct may be updated as Ara and its community evolve.
