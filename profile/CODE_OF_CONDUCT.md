# Ara Code of Conduct

Ara is building agents that help people get real work done. Our community should be practical, kind, and safe for contributors, customers, partners, and anyone who joins our public spaces.

## Our values

We expect everyone participating in Ara projects and spaces to:

- Be respectful, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome people across backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Share feedback in ways that help the work and the people improve.
- Protect user privacy, security, confidential information, and consent.
- Keep discussions focused on building useful, trustworthy software.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Giving clear, actionable feedback without personal attacks.
- Respecting boundaries when someone asks to pause or move a discussion.
- Disclosing conflicts of interest when they matter to a decision.
- Reporting security or safety concerns through the right channel instead of amplifying harm publicly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment and abuse are not tolerated. Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

- Offensive comments related to age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, socioeconomic status, or other protected characteristics.
- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances in community spaces.
- Threats, intimidation, stalking, doxxing, sustained disruption, or deliberate attempts to make someone feel unsafe.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or repeated bad-faith engagement.
- Publishing private communications or personal information without explicit permission.
- Encouraging others to violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or if you have a community safety concern, report it to Ara by emailing conduct@ara.so.

Please include as much context as you can safely share, such as:

- What happened and when.
- Where it happened, including links or screenshots when appropriate.
- Who was involved or affected.
- Whether there is an immediate safety, privacy, or security risk.

Ara will handle reports with care and discretion. We will limit details to the people who need them to review, respond, and protect the community. If a report involves someone responsible for enforcement, Ara will route the review to another appropriate decision-maker.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through Ara's security reporting process when one is available for the affected repository or product. Community safety concerns should still use conduct@ara.so.

## Enforcement guidelines

Ara maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate and proportionate, including:

1. Private clarification, coaching, or a written warning.
2. Requiring a correction, apology, or retraction.
3. Removing comments, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions.
4. Temporary restrictions from repositories, community channels, events, or programs.
5. Permanent removal from Ara community spaces.
6. Escalation to platform providers, legal counsel, or emergency services when required to protect people or comply with the law.

Enforcement decisions should consider severity, impact, intent, prior behavior, and risk of recurrence. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is itself a violation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Ara repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, community channels, events, and other spaces where someone is representing Ara or participating in Ara-related work.
