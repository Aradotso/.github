Ara is a Mac-native AI agent that designs, builds, runs, and refines automated agents directly on your computer. It controls your screen, browser, and local tools — and learns from every run.

**What Ara does:**

- Runs background coding agents in isolated git worktrees
- Controls your Mac (screen, keyboard, mouse, apps)
- Browses the web and interacts with live pages
- Connects to your dev stack: GitHub, Linear, Supabase, Vercel, Stripe, and more
- Remembers what works and saves reusable skills across sessions

**Get started:** [ara.so](https://www.ara.so)
