Ara is the self-driving IDE for product development. It finds the next improvement to your product and codebase, ships it, learns the method as a skill, and starts the next loop — without waiting for a prompt.

**Find work. Ship it. Learn. Repeat.**

Ara is backed by Y Combinator and used by builders at Anthropic, Perplexity, Apple, Meta, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and more.

---

## What Ara does

**It finds the next improvement on its own.**
The loop never waits for another prompt. Ara reads your product and codebase, spots the highest-value next step, and turns it into a scheduled agent or background run — so work keeps moving while you're elsewhere.

**It ships the change and learns the method.**
Ara runs the work end to end: it edits files, drives the browser and terminal, opens a PR, reviews the diff, then keeps the method as a skill. Every run leaves the loop sharper than it found it.

**It remembers, then starts the next loop.**
Memory, skills, and Mac computer use close the loop: Ara remembers what shipped, understands the codebase a little better, and tees up the next improvement. Self-driving, and compounding.

---

## Who it's for

Ara is built for developers, founders, and technical teams who want their codebase to keep moving — not just when they're at the keyboard, but continuously.
