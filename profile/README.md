**About Ara**

Software development is changing faster than the tools built to support it. Most developer tools still assume the bottleneck is writing code — but with AI, that bottleneck has shifted upstream, to planning: deciding what to build, breaking it into tasks, and keeping the whole system coherent as it evolves. Ara is built for that world. We're building the IDE for the future, where agents handle the execution and the developer's job is high-level direction — setting goals, reviewing outcomes, and steering.

The core insight behind Ara is that proactive planning is the missing layer in AI-assisted development. Today's tools react when you ask them to. Ara's agents get ahead of the work: they monitor your repo, surface what needs attention, propose what to tackle next, and execute autonomously inside isolated workspaces — so nothing falls through the cracks between your intent and the finished diff.

Ara was founded by [Sven Myhre](https://github.com/svemyh) and Adi Singh, both with EECS backgrounds and a shared conviction that the developer experience needs a fundamental rethink. We're backed by Y Combinator — you can find us on the [YC company page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ara) and follow our progress there.
