Ara supercharges your workflows with intelligent automation, deep system integration, and a proactive AI that works the way you think.

Summon Ara from anywhere on your Mac with a single keystroke. It reads the room — your open apps, recent files, calendar, and clipboard — and takes action before you even ask. From reviewing pull requests to drafting emails to searching your knowledge base, Ara doesn't just answer: it does.

**Built for power users, developers, and founders** who demand speed, precision, and control.

---

### Why Ara

- **Proactive Intelligence** — Ara anticipates your next move and surfaces suggestions, summaries, and actions before you ask.
- **Instant Access** — One keystroke from any app. No context-switching, no interruptions.
- **Deep Integrations** — Works with the tools you already use: Notion, Linear, Slack, GitHub, and more.
- **Context-Aware** — Understands what you're working on and adapts in real time.
- **Privacy First** — Your data stays local unless you choose to share it.
- **Fully Customizable** — Build your own workflows, shortcuts, and automations.
