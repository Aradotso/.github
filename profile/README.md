---

Ara lives on your Mac, sees what you're doing across apps and windows, and handles the tasks you keep putting off.

It isn't a chatbot you switch to — it's a layer on top of everything you already do. Ara monitors your screen, audio, and activity to understand your context, then acts on it without you having to copy-paste, explain, or context-switch.

**Awareness.** Ara is always watching and listening, so you don't have to ask. It understands what's happening across every app, invisibly.

**Action.** Ara automates your Mac end-to-end — filling forms, browsing the web, managing files, writing and executing code — all in the background.

**Memory.** Ara learns your preferences, habits, and context over time. Your brain lives locally on your device; Ara learns who you work with, what tools you use, and how you like to get things done.

**Intelligence.** Ara isn't locked to one model. Plug in Claude, Gemini, or any other AI and use it across every task.
