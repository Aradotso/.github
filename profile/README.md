**Ara** is an AI agent that lives on your Mac and builds, runs, and refines other agents for you. It controls your desktop, browser, and terminal — so your automations can do real work in real apps, not just chat windows.

Ara is backed by [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com) and built by [Sven Myhre](https://github.com/svemyh) and Adi Singh.

### What we're building

- **Background agents** — Ara spins up isolated coding agents in git worktrees, lets them ship PRs, then merges and deploys automatically.
- **Computer use** — agents that see your screen, click, type, and navigate just like you would.
- **Persistent memory** — facts, preferences, and learned workflows that carry across every session.
- **Agent skills** — a growing library of reusable procedures Ara writes and improves as it works.
